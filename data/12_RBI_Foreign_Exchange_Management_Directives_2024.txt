FOREIGN EXCHANGE MANAGEMENT ACT (FEMA) - RBI MASTER DIRECTION ON OVERSEAS DIRECT INVESTMENT (ODI)
Notification No. FEMA 400/2022-RB (Updated March 2024)

Rule 9: Limit on Financial Commitment by Indian Entity
1. Total financial commitment made by an Indian entity in all foreign entities shall not exceed 400% of its net worth as per the last audited balance sheet.
2. Net Worth Calculation: Paid-up equity capital + Free reserves + Securities premium - Accumulated losses - Deferred expenditure.
3. Permissible Modes: Equity, Debt instruments, Loan, Guarantee (Corporate, Personal, Bank), Pledge of shares/assets.
