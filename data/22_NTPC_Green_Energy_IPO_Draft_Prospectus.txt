NTPC GREEN ENERGY LIMITED - DRAFT RED HERRING PROSPECTUS (DRHP) SUMMARY
Objects of the Initial Public Offering (IPO)

1. Issue Size: Fresh Issue of Equity Shares aggregating up to INR 10,000 Crores.
2. Utilization of Net Proceeds:
- Repayment/Prepayment of certain outstanding borrowings of subsidiary (NTPC Renewable Energy Ltd): INR 7,500 Crores.
- General Corporate Purposes: INR 2,500 Crores.

3. Operational Capacity:
- Operational Renewable Capacity: 3,071 MW (Solar: 2,950 MW, Wind: 121 MW).
- Pipeline / Contracted Capacity under construction: 11,200 MW.
- Power Purchase Agreements (PPA) Weighted Average Tariff: INR 2.68 per kWh with average PPA duration of 25 years.
