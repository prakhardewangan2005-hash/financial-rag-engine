SUN PHARMACEUTICAL INDUSTRIES LIMITED - REGULATORY UPDATE ON US FDA INSPECTION
Facility: Halol Manufacturing Plant & Mohali Oral Solid Dosage Facility

1. Mohali Facility Status:
- US FDA completed inspection between June 10-18, 2024.
- Issued Form FDA 483 with 5 procedural observations.
- Observations relate to environmental monitoring in aseptic packaging lines and batch record documentation.
- Facility currently under Import Alert 66-40 with exemptions for 14 life-saving oncology formulations.

2. Financial Provisioning:
Revenue contribution from Mohali facility to US sales: ~3.5% (approx USD 65 Million). No asset impairment charged to P&L.
