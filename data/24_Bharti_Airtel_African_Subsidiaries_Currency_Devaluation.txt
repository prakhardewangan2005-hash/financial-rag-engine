BHARTI AIRTEL LIMITED - IMPACT OF NIGERIAN NAIRA DEVALUATION ON CONSOLIDATED RESULTS
Audited Financial Notes FY24 - Note 41

1. Foreign Exchange Devaluation:
The Central Bank of Nigeria unified exchange rate windows leading to a devaluation of the Nigerian Naira (NGN) against USD by over 100% during the year.

2. Impact on Profit Before Tax:
- Exceptional forex loss recognized: INR 5,420 Crores in consolidated P&L.
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) in Nigeria (in USD terms) dropped from $4.8 to $2.2, while constant currency local ARPU grew by 11.4%.
