ITC LIMITED - SCHEME OF ARRANGEMENT FOR DEMERGER OF HOTEL BUSINESS
National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) Kolkata Filing

1. Share Entitlement Ratio:
For every 10 (Ten) Ordinary Shares of face value of Re. 1/- each held in ITC Limited, 1 (One) Equity Share of face value of Re. 1/- each in ITC Hotels Limited shall be issued.

2. Post-Demerger Shareholding Pattern of ITC Hotels Limited:
- Directly held by existing shareholders of ITC Limited: 60%.
- Held by ITC Limited (Parent entity): 40%.
